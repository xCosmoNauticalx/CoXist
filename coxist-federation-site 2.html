The Federation of Worker Cooperatives

Built by workers. Owned by workers.

Co-Xist is a federation of independent worker cooperatives — a network where founders join as equal co-owners, share infrastructure and protections, and keep what they build. Two doors in. Pick yours.

Path 02 · Founder Survey · ~3 minutes

Tell us what you're building.

Seven questions. Honest answers. We match you to the right structure (single coop or full federation membership), the right operational support, and the right capital path.

QUESTION 01
Who are we talking to?

Your name and the company you're building (or thinking about building).

QUESTION 02
What does your company actually do?

One sentence. No jargon. If you can't say it in a sentence, that's useful information too.

QUESTION 03
Do you want to join as a single coop, or as a federation member?

The difference is structural. Read both — the right answer depends on where you're headed.

QUESTION 04
Which worker benefits do you want to offer?

Pick everything you'd want to provide. The federation pools these so even tiny companies can offer enterprise-grade benefits.

QUESTION 05
What operational help do you want?

Pick everything that would actually move the needle for you. We'll quote you the right package based on your selection.

QUESTION 06
Where are you in the journey?

Honest stage. We don't grade you on it — we use it to recommend the right onboarding track.

QUESTION 07
How big is your team today?

Including you. Founders count.
